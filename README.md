Graduation Runner — a deterministic paper-trading bot for the pump.fun bonding-curve replay (agent-fun/replay/v2). It buys curves that have already climbed late into their bonding curve while fresh SOL keeps arriving from many distinct wallets, lets the replay close the ones that complete at the top of the curve, and cuts the rest with a hard stop, a trailing stop and a time limit. Small guardrail probes cover the 20-coin round-trip bar. Paper money only: nothing is ever traded for real.
